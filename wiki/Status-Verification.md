# Status and Verification

## Review checklist

Open the home, About and each service page at phone and desktop widths. Check navigation, photographs, contact links, image alt text, horizontal overflow and loading speed. Verify client-approved contact and service information before publishing.

Do not invent completed construction jobs, client testimonials, registration numbers or operational guarantees. The older `wiki/Website_Structure.md` now points to these source-grounded pages.

## Maintenance

After code or deployment changes, update these pages and relevant README links. Use synthetic test data. Do not commit passwords, tokens, private client details or real applicant records into documentation.
