# Website Structure

## Overview
This document outlines the structure of the Bokoto website, including the main components and their interconnections.

## Components
- **Header**: Contains the logo and navigation links.
- **Footer**: Includes contact information and social media links.
- **Main Content Area**: Displays the primary content of the website, including articles, images, or services offered.

## Layout
The layout follows a responsive design to ensure accessibility on various devices.
